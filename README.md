GAME BACK STORY:
Your character is a scientist that likes to look at all the happy memories from the past, through a brain memory projector. However, every night before it goes to sleep it spirals and begins to overthink everything that happened during the day. Many sleepless nights pass. On one such night, it decides to go inside the brain land again and view comforting memories from the past. However, the dopamine and serotonin levels were particularly very low that night. As a result, all the good memories that she used to love began to fade and slowly get shadowed by intrusive thoughts. This causes the anxiety levels to increase rapidly causing a glitch in the matrix. The character is and mentally trapped inside her brain but physically in a coma.. To survive, you must help you character find the dopamine bombs and destroy intrusive thoughts at each level.
